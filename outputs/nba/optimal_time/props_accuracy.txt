props accuracy

This uses every settled market in the optimal-time sample, not the top-10 chart sample. At each five-minute mark, mean win chance is the average mid and actual win rate is the share of those markets whose charted outcome happened. Each market votes once.
Relative accuracy is 1 minus the absolute gap between those two. Profit at a mark is the average profit of buying the side that paid 1, about 1 minus the winner's price. It is large when the winner was cheap and shrinks as that price moves toward 1. Profit score rescales that profit from 0 at the worst eligible mark to 1 at the best. The joint score is half profit score and half relative accuracy. A mark needs at least 8 markets before it can win.
