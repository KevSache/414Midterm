How often the price called the outcome

The win chance on a five-minute row is that row's mid. A mid of 0.70 is a 70% chance the charted outcome happens. A call predicts it above 0.5 and predicts it does not happen below 0.5. A mid of exactly 0.5 is not a call.
One market has one result, so an hour spent at the same price is twelve rows and still one market. The tables count each market once inside a five-minute mark or a price band.

Bin-level calls: 55.8% of 1176 quotes matched the final result (12 markets). This counts a long quote path more than once.
Bin-level Brier score: 0.254. This is the average of (win chance − outcome) squared. A coin flip scores 0.250.
Across five-minute marks, the average call hit rate is 55.6%. Each mark counts once, so one market quoted all night does not dominate this average.
